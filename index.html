WHAT WE DO

Intelligent Solutions
for Every Farm

From soil to sky, ShambaPoint provides end-to-end intelligence that helps farmers make smarter decisions and achieve sustainable growth across Kenya's diverse agro-climatic zones.

Micro-Climate Forecasts

Daily dry-spell risk scores and 7-day hyper-local rain forecasts for individual farms — not just counties. Know your field's forecast, not just the region's.

Learn more →

Smart Irrigation Advice

AI-driven recommendations on exactly when and how much to water, calibrated to your soil type, crop stage, and the upcoming forecast — saving water and protecting yields.

Learn more →

Yield Registry & Matching

Farmers register expected harvests before the season ends. Our marketplace automatically matches surviving yields to verified B2B buyers — supermarkets, hotels, processors and exporters.

Learn more →

Logistics & M-Pesa Payment

Book trucks, track deliveries, and receive M-Pesa payment automatically released on confirmed delivery — zero cash handling, zero delayed payments.

Learn more →
